ʿAqīdah

The foundation of Islamic faith — understanding who Allah is, His attributes, and what it means to worship Him alone.

⭐ All Levels 📖 Integrated across programmes 🕌 Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jamāʿah
⭐

The Foundation of Faith

ʿAqīdah (Islamic creed) is the foundation upon which all Islamic practice and ethics are built. It encompasses the core beliefs that every Muslim must hold — belief in Allah, His angels, His revealed books, His messengers, the Day of Judgement, and divine decree (qadar), both its good and its challenging aspects.

At FIKD Foundation, we teach ʿAqīdah according to the understanding of Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jamāʿah, grounding students in authentic sources: the Qurʾān, the Sunnah, and the consensus of the early generations (Salaf). Our approach is both deeply rooted and accessible, addressing contemporary questions and doubts with clarity and conviction.

  • Rooted in Qurʾān and Sunnah
  • Clear, reasoned approach to theology
  • Addressing modern misconceptions
  • Strengthening faith and certainty

What You'll Study

Key Topics in ʿAqīdah

❖
🤲
01

Tawḥīd (Oneness of Allah)

  • Tawḥīd al-Rubūbiyyah (Oneness of Lordship)
  • Tawḥīd al-Ulūhiyyah (Oneness of Worship)
  • Tawḥīd al-Asmāʾ wa al-Ṣifāt
  • Implications of Tawḥīd in daily life
👼
02

Belief in Angels

  • Nature and attributes of angels
  • Their functions and roles
  • Key angels: Jibrīl, Mīkāʾīl, Isrāfīl
  • Distinction from other beings
📖
03

Belief in the Books

  • The Qurʾān as the final revelation
  • Previous scriptures: Tawrāh, Zabūr, Injīl
  • The Qurʾān's preservation and miracles
🕊️
04

Belief in the Messengers

  • Prophethood — its nature and necessity
  • Stories of key prophets
  • Finality of Prophethood (Khatm al-Nubuwwah)
⚖️
05

Belief in the Hereafter

  • Death, grave, and Barzakh
  • The Day of Judgement
  • Paradise (Jannah) and Hellfire (Jahannam)
📿
06

Divine Decree (Qadar)

  • Allah's knowledge, will, and creation
  • Human free will and responsibility
  • Contentment with qadar
