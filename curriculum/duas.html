Duʿāʾ

The essence of worship — learning the art of supplication, its etiquettes, and the beautiful duʿāʾ from the Qurʾān and Sunnah.

🤲 All Levels ⏱ Integrated across programmes 📖 Qurʾān & Sunnah
🤲

The Weapon of the Believer

Duʿāʾ (supplication) is the essence of worship — it is the believer's direct line of communication with Allah. The Prophet ﷺ said, "Duʿāʾ is worship" (Tirmidhī). Through duʿāʾ, we express our dependence on Allah, seek His forgiveness, ask for His guidance, and pour out our hearts to Him.

At FIKD Foundation, we study duʿāʾ not just as a set of words to memorise, but as a spiritual practice that transforms the heart. Students learn the etiquettes of supplication, the best times and states for duʿāʾ, and the beautiful supplications taught by the Prophet ﷺ and mentioned in the Qurʾān. This subject aims to turn duʿāʾ from a ritual into a living, vibrant connection with the Creator.

  • Mastering the etiquettes and conditions of duʿāʾ
  • Learning key duʿāʾ from the Qurʾān and Sunnah
  • Understanding the best times and places for supplication
  • Developing a sincere, heartfelt connection with Allah

What You'll Study

Key Topics in Duʿāʾ

❖
📖
01

Definition & Significance

  • What is duʿāʾ? Its linguistic and Islamic meaning
  • The importance of duʿāʾ in the Qurʾān and Ḥadīth
  • Duʿāʾ as the essence of worship
  • The relationship between duʿāʾ, tawakkul, and action
✨
02

Etiquettes & Conditions

  • Sincerity (ikhlāṣ) and presence of heart
  • Beginning with praise of Allah and ṣalāh upon the Prophet ﷺ
  • Being in a state of purity and facing the qiblah
  • Raising the hands, humility, and persistence
  • Having certainty in Allah's response
⏰
03

Times & Places of Acceptance

  • Duʿāʾ in ṣalāh and sujūd (prostration)
  • Between adhān and iqāmah
  • During the last third of the night
  • On Friday (the hour of acceptance)
  • During rain, travel, and while fasting
📜
04

Selected Duʿāʾ from the Qurʾān

  • Duʿāʾ of the prophets (Ādam, Nūḥ, Ibrāhīm, Mūsā, etc.)
  • Duʿāʾ for guidance, forgiveness, and mercy
  • Duʿāʾ for patience, victory, and provision
  • Sūrah al-Fātiḥah as the greatest duʿāʾ
🕌
05

Prophetic Duʿāʾ (Adʿiyyah)

  • Morning and evening adhkār (remembrances)
  • Duʿāʾ before eating, sleeping, entering/leaving the home
  • Duʿāʾ for protection from evil and disease
  • Duʿāʾ for parents, family, and the community
  • Comprehensive duʿāʾ (e.g., duʿāʾ of Yunus, duʿāʾ of distress)
❤️
06

Living with Duʿāʾ

  • Making duʿāʾ a daily habit
  • Understanding the etiquette of asking from Allah
  • Dealing with unanswered duʿāʾ – patience and wisdom
  • Teaching children the importance of duʿāʾ
