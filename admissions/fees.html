Payment

Payment Options

❖

Choose the payment plan that works best for you and your family.

💳
01

Full Payment

Pay the full term fee upfront and receive a 5% discount on your total. Ideal for students who prefer a single payment.

📅
02

Monthly Installments

Spread your payments over the duration of the term with interest-free monthly installments. Flexible options available.

🎓
03

Scholarship Support

Need-based and merit-based scholarships available for eligible students. Apply for financial aid during the application process.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
04

Family Discount

Families with multiple students receive a 10% discount on total fees. Applicable to immediate family members.

Financial Aid

Scholarships & Financial Aid

❖

We believe that financial constraints should never be a barrier to seeking knowledge. Explore our support options below.

🤲

Need-Based Scholarships

For students facing financial hardship. Apply with a simple statement of need, and we'll review your case compassionately.

⭐

Merit-Based Awards

Outstanding students can receive scholarships based on academic performance, dedication, and progress in their studies.

🕌

Community Sponsorship

We partner with donors and community organizations to sponsor students who demonstrate commitment to Islamic learning.

💬 Questions about financial aid? Contact our admissions team — we're here to help.
