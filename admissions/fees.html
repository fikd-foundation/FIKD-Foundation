Programme Fees

Affordable, transparent pricing designed to make authentic Islamic education accessible to everyone.

💰 Flexible Payment Plans 🎓 Scholarships Available 🌍 Worldwide
Investment

Fee Structure

Our fees are structured to provide excellent value for quality Islamic education. We offer flexible payment options to suit your needs.

Programme Duration Sessions/Week Fee (per term) Fee (per month)
Qāʿidah – Beginner 12 weeks 2 $149 $50
Qāʿidah – Upper 14 weeks 2 $199 $57
Qurʾān – Beginner 16 weeks 2 $249 $62
Qurʾān – Upper 24 weeks (2 terms) 3 $349 $58

* All fees are in USD. Payment plans available.

Payment

Payment Options

💳

Full Payment

Pay the full term fee upfront and receive a 5% discount on your total. Ideal for students who prefer a single payment.

📅

Monthly Installments

Spread your payments over the duration of the term with interest-free monthly installments. Flexible options available.

🎓

Scholarship Support

Need-based and merit-based scholarships available for eligible students. Apply for financial aid during the application process.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Discount

Families with multiple students receive a 10% discount on total fees. Applicable to immediate family members.
