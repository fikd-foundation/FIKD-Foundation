How to Apply

Admissions Process

Our streamlined admissions process is designed to make it easy for you to start your learning journey.

1

Submit Application

Fill out our online application form with your details and programme preferences.

2

Placement Assessment

For Qurʾān programmes, we conduct a brief assessment to determine your level.

3

Enrolment & Orientation

Once accepted, you'll be enrolled and receive an orientation to get started.

4

Begin Your Studies

Join your first class and embark on a transformative Islamic learning journey.

Eligibility

Admissions Requirements

📝

General Requirements

  • Commitment to learning and personal growth
  • Access to a stable internet connection
  • Basic computer literacy
  • Willingness to engage with course material
📖

Qāʿidah Programmes

  • No prior knowledge required
  • Open to all ages (children and adults)
  • Ability to commit to weekly classes
🕌

Qurʾān Programmes

  • Completion of Qāʿidah or equivalent
  • Ability to read Arabic script
  • Assessment for appropriate level placement
🌍

International Students

  • All nationalities welcome
  • Classes available in multiple time zones
  • Language support available
