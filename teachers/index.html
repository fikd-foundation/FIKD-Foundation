Our Faculty

Qualified, Experienced, & Dedicated

Every teacher at FIKD Foundation is a qualified scholar with formal training in Islamic sciences. Many hold Ijāzah (chains of transmission) in their respective fields, ensuring that the knowledge passed on to our students is authentic and unbroken.
