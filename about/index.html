Our Mission

To provide authentic, accessible, and transformative Islamic education that nurtures faith, knowledge, and character — empowering individuals to live Islam with confidence and contribute positively to their communities.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a global beacon of Islamic learning, where every student finds a pathway to connect with the Qurʾān and Sunnah, and where traditional scholarship meets contemporary relevance.

Our Journey

From a Dream to a Global Community

❖

What started as a small study circle has grown into a worldwide movement of authentic Islamic learning.

2016

Foundation Launched

FIKD began as a small study circle in Mardan with just 12 students, driven by a passion to share authentic Islamic knowledge.

2018

First Online Programme

We expanded beyond the classroom, offering our first online Qāʿidah course — making learning accessible worldwide.

2021

Full Curriculum Launch

With the addition of new teachers and subjects, we rolled out a comprehensive 7‑pillar curriculum covering ʿAqīdah, Ḥadīth, Sīrah, and more.

2024

Global Reach

Today, FIKD serves over 1,200 students from more than 30 countries, with a dedicated team of 48 qualified teachers.

2026

Looking Ahead

We continue to grow, improve, and reach more families — always committed to authentic Islamic education for all.

Core Values

What We Stand For

❖

These principles guide everything we do at FIKD.

📿
01

Authenticity

We adhere to the Qurʾān and Sunnah as understood by the righteous predecessors (Salaf), with respect for classical scholarship.

🧠
02

Excellence

We strive for the highest quality in teaching, materials, and student support, continuously improving our programmes.

🤝
03

Community

We foster a supportive, inclusive environment where students and teachers grow together in faith and knowledge.

🌱
04

Character Development

Knowledge without action is incomplete; we emphasise adab (manners) and tazkiyah (purification) alongside study.
