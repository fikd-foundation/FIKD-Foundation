Quran Naazera Programme

A comprehensive, structured journey from foundational Noorani Qaida to advanced Qurʾānic recitation with tajwīd, memorisation, and deep Islamic understanding.

📖 6 Levels 🕌 Complete Tajwīd 🌐 Live Online 👧 For All Ages & Genders
Quran Naazera

Complete Recitation Programme

❖

From Foundation to Excellence — A structured journey through the Qurʾān with integrated Islamic knowledge, tajwīd, and character development.

Qurʾān – Level 1

Foundation

Learn Noorani Qaida basics: Arabic alphabet, vowels, and basic reading skills. Build a strong foundation for Qurʾānic recitation.

Intermediate
