Qurʾān – Upper

Mastery of the Qurʾān – advanced recitation, complete tajwīd, in-depth Tafsīr, and full memorisation. The pinnacle of our Qurʾānic programmes.

🕋 Level 4 · Advanced ⏱ 24 weeks (2 terms) 🌐 Live Online
🕋

Excellence in Qurʾānic Studies

The Qurʾān – Upper programme is the culmination of our Qurʾānic track, designed for students who have completed the Qurʾān – Beginner programme or have equivalent proficiency. This course focuses on achieving mastery in all aspects of Qurʾānic study: flawless recitation with advanced tajwīd, comprehensive memorisation of the entire Qurʾān (or selected portions), and a deep understanding of the text through Tafsīr, linguistic analysis (Iʿrāb), and thematic studies.

Students work closely with our most senior teachers, many of whom hold Ijāzah in the ten qirāʾāt. The programme is intensive but flexible, allowing students to progress at a pace that suits their goals — whether that means completing the full ḥifẓ or achieving mastery in recitation and Tafsīr.

  • Mastery of all tajwīd rules and qirāʾāt
  • Full or partial ḥifẓ (memorisation) with Ijāzah pathway
  • In-depth Tafsīr and Iʿrāb (grammatical analysis)
  • Personalised mentorship from senior scholars

What You'll Learn

Curriculum Overview

Module 1: Advanced Tajwīd & Qirāʾāt

  • Complete mastery of all tajwīd rules with practical application
  • Study of the ten qirāʾāt (canonical recitations) – differences and principles
  • Advanced madd rules, waqf, and ibtidāʾ (starting/stopping)
  • Recitation with full fluency and beauty (tartīl and taḥqīq)

Module 2: Ḥifẓ (Memorisation)

  • Systematic memorisation of the entire Qurʾān (or customised portions)
  • Advanced memorisation techniques and revision strategies
  • Regular testing and feedback from teachers
  • Pathway to obtaining Ijāzah in ḥifẓ

Module 3: Tafsīr & Qurʾānic Sciences

  • In-depth study of selected sūrahs and their Tafsīr
  • Thematic analysis of Qurʾānic concepts (e.g., Tawḥīd, Prophethood, Akhirāh)
  • Introduction to ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān (sciences of the Qurʾān)
  • Study of Asbāb al-Nuzūl (reasons for revelation)

Module 4: Iʿrāb & Linguistic Analysis

  • Grammatical analysis (Iʿrāb) of selected verses
  • Understanding Arabic syntax and morphology in Qurʾānic context
  • Connecting linguistic structure to meaning
  • Practical application in recitation and understanding
By the End

What You Will Achieve

✅

Recite the entire Qurʾān with flawless tajwīd and mastery of qirāʾāt.

✅

Memorise the full Qurʾān (or a substantial portion) with confidence.

✅

Understand the Qurʾān through in-depth Tafsīr and thematic analysis.

✅

Analyse Qurʾānic Arabic grammatically and connect it to meaning.

Before You Start

Prerequisites

This programme is designed for advanced students who have completed the Qurʾān – Beginner programme or have equivalent skills. You should be able to:

  • Recite the Qurʾān fluently with proper tajwīd
  • Have memorised Juz ʿAmma (or equivalent portion)
  • Understand basic Tafsīr and Arabic vocabulary
  • Demonstrate a strong commitment to intensive study

If you're unsure about your level, contact us for a free assessment.
